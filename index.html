About Nikhol

Some things are better said out loud.

I’m a speaker, facilitator, well-being advocate, and author. I’ve spent more than two decades bringing people together around conversations that matter.

My breast cancer experience became Book of Boobs. The conversation grew from there: life after treatment, menopause, changing identities, caregiving, and what comes next.

I bring lived experience, a background in improv, and a willingness to say the quiet part out loud. I don’t pretend to have all the answers. I do make room for the truth, and a little laughter.

Speaker bio

Nikhol J. Atkins is a speaker, facilitator, well-being advocate, and author of Book of Boobs: A Visual Journey Through Breast Cancer. With more than two decades of experience bringing people together and a background in improv, she creates space for humor, hope, and honest conversation about well-being, leadership, and life’s transitions.

She holds a Bachelor of Science in Healthcare Systems Administration; a Master of Arts in General Administration with a concentration in Strategic Leadership; and a Master of Arts in Spirituality, Culture & Health. She also holds a graduate certificate in Approaches to Spirituality & Healing and certifications in yoga and meditation.
